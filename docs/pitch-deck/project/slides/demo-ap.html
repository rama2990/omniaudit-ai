This is one bill from the demo scenario. The contractor bills 80%, the site inspection report says 75%, and the project is 10 days past its target date. The system recalculates from the inspected progress, deducts down-payment recoupment, retention and the late penalty, and applies the withholding tax rate for the contractor’s grade. Net payable drops from 2.575 billion to 2.113 billion rupiah. That 462 million gap is exactly what slips through a rushed manual check. Because the claim is above 500 million, the system locks the bill and routes it to the CFO.
