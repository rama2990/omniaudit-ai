The CFO stays in control, and external auditors can trace everything

Tiered approval

Disputes above IDR 500 million, hard stops and executive waivers go to the CFO’s queue.

Data isolated per SPV

Payables and receivables staff only see their assigned SPVs, enforced on every endpoint.

Hash-chained audit trail

Every override, approval and waiver is logged. Changes made outside the app are detected.

Measured AI accuracy

Every human correction of an AI-read value is counted per field, to evaluate the model.

The CFO has a dedicated room: large decisions, bills that were stopped outright, and executive waivers. Every action lands in an audit trail where each entry is hash-linked to the previous one, so a direct database edit is caught. In the demo, the Simulate tampering button shows the chain breaking. Human corrections of what the AI read are tracked as an accuracy metric.
