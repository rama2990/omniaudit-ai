The impact targets come from the product requirements: auditing a bill drops from 3 to 5 working days to under 15 minutes, no overbilling gets through, and 95 percent of buyer payments are allocated on the same day. These are targets, not results on real data; the first step is a pilot in one SPV. Integrations that are simulated today, such as ERP, bank feeds and WhatsApp, are the next production work.
