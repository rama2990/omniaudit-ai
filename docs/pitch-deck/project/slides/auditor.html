The adversarial auditor is a rule engine, not an LLM. Eight checks run on every bill. High-severity findings lock the bill as a dispute; critical ones, like the same bill sent to two SPVs, stop it completely and only the CFO can release it. Medium findings are corrected automatically and the bill can still be approved with a yellow flag. Construction withholding tax follows Indonesian Government Regulation 9/2022, based on the contractor’s grade in the vendor master, not on the rate the contractor wrote.
