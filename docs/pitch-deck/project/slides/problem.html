Money leaks both ways, in every SPV

Contractor bills

Invoices claim more progress than the site inspection report. Late penalties, 5% retention and down-payment recoupment get missed. Manual checks take 3 to 5 working days.

Buyer payments

Transfers read “BLK C1/02”, the payer differs from the name on the sales contract, or bank fees leave it short. Money sits unallocated and late fees go unrecorded.

Many SPVs

Every project is a separate company. The same invoice can be billed to two SPVs, and journals are easy to misallocate.

The problem isn’t headcount. It’s too many numbers matched by hand before money goes out or comes in.

There are two leak points. Going out, contractors bill progress that hasn’t happened yet, and mandatory deductions such as retention, penalties and down-payment recoupment slip through manual checks. Coming in, thousands of buyer transfers can’t be matched to their unit right away. Running many SPVs adds risk: the same bill charged to two companies, and journals posted to the wrong one.
